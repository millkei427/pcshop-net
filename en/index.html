PC Shop

The company runs a retail division, a software development division and a systems solutions division — making it one of the few IT companies in Macau able to deliver hardware and software integration under one roof.

We currently serve more than 20 well-known Macau institutions, including the University of Macau, the General Union of Neighbourhood Associations and numerous primary and secondary schools, across education, telecommunications, healthcare, hospitality and finance — a trusted IT partner to Macau businesses and organisations.

Our Story

Our Story

Since 2007 we have grown from an IT hardware and software supplier into a one-stop technology company with retail, software development and systems solutions divisions, serving businesses across Macau for over 18 years.

2007

PC Shop founded

Founded in Macau supplying IT hardware and software, with a retail division serving individuals and businesses with computer products and repairs — and quickly building a reputation.

2010

Low-voltage engineering begins

We took on more low-voltage engineering work — network cabling, surveillance systems and AV installation — steadily earning the trust of corporate clients.

2015

Expanding our services

We established our software development and systems solutions divisions, supplying information systems and custom software, and became one of Macau’s few one-stop IT integrators.

2022

Online shop launched

We launched our online shop with 149+ genuine products from brands including ASUS and Lenovo, introduced SMARTBoard interactive whiteboards, and began delivering across Macau.

Today

Still growing

We help businesses and schools put AI to practical use in day-to-day operations, improving efficiency and competitiveness. Our clients span many sectors — including the University of Macau, CTM (Companhia de Telecomunicações de Macau), the General Union of Neighbourhood Associations, primary and secondary schools and healthcare institutions — as we continue to support Macau’s digital transformation.
