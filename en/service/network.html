Frequently asked questions

Our office has Wi-Fi dead spots. What can be done?

Usually a single router simply cannot cover the space. We measure the signal on site and recommend either a mesh setup (TP-Link Deco, for instance) or additional access points, so coverage is seamless. The assessment is free.

We're fitting out a new shop. When should the cabling be done?

Ideally while the walls are still open during fit-out — that avoids surface trunking and channelling later. Send us the floor plan on WhatsApp and we can assess it in advance.

Can I supply my own equipment?

Yes, we will still install and configure it. Buying it with the work is usually cheaper, and it keeps warranty and support with one company.

Do you install security cameras too?

Yes. We supply and install TP-Link Tapo indoor and outdoor cameras and video doorbells, and they can be planned together with the network work.

Need network engineering & wi-fi installation?
