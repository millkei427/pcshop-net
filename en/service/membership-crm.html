Why use a membership CRM?

Many shops welcome new customers every day, but lose them after a while. The reason isn't poor service — it's the lack of a system to follow up:

  • Customer data is scattered across Excel files, WhatsApp chats and paper records
  • Birthday offers, treatment expiry and lapsed-customer follow-ups depend entirely on staff memory
  • When running promotions, you don't know which customers are most valuable and send offers at random
  • Membership points are calculated incorrectly and gift redemption is cumbersome, leading to complaints

A CRM helps you centralise your data and automate your workflows, so staff no longer have to remember anything — the system records it automatically.

FAQ

How is a membership CRM system different from a POS?

A POS system mainly handles checkout transactions, while a CRM focuses on managing customer relationships: recording spending preferences, following up on return visits and sending offers automatically. The two can be integrated, with transaction data written automatically into member profiles.

Can existing member data be imported?

Yes. We help you organise Excel files or data from your old system, clean up the formats and import everything into the new system to ensure the data is complete.

Can the system send offers automatically?

Yes. You can set up rules such as birthday offers, follow-ups for customers who have not visited in a while, automatic coupon issuance once a spending threshold is reached, and upgrade privileges. The system triggers them automatically via WhatsApp / Email / SMS.

How many members do we need before a CRM is worth using?

Generally speaking, you start seeing results once you have more than 100 active members. What matters is not the headcount, but how much manual follow-up you want automation to eliminate and how much you want to raise your repurchase rate.
