Which Companies Need an ERP?

  • Daily purchasing, sales and inventory activity, with reconciliation done in Excel
  • POS at the front line and accounting software in the back office, with no data flowing between them
  • Multiple branches or departments, with head office wanting real-time visibility of each unit
  • Reports have to wait for staff to compile them manually, so decisions lag behind
  • Inaccurate inventory, leading to frequent stockouts or overstocking

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between ERP and POS?

POS mainly handles front-line cashiering, while ERP covers overall back-office operations: purchasing, inventory, finance, sales and reporting. The two can be integrated, so POS transaction data flows automatically into the ERP and inventory and finances stay in sync in real time.

Can old data be imported into the new ERP?

Yes. We help organise your existing Excel files, legacy system or accounting software data, clean up the formats and import it into the new system, and arrange a parallel trial run to ensure accuracy.

How large does a company need to be before it needs an ERP?

As a rule of thumb, companies with more than 10 staff, daily purchasing, inventory and sales activity, or more than 20 hours of reconciliation per month start to see results. The key is reducing duplicate entry and manual reconciliation.

Can it integrate with my existing accounting software?

Yes. We design API or report-based integration to suit your existing system, for example exporting ERP data to your accounting software, or syncing customer and supplier data both ways.
