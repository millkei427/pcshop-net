When do you need an App or a mini program?

  • Your customers mainly interact on mobile and want one-tap booking, ordering or enquiries
  • You want to reach customers in Mainland China, Hong Kong and Macau through the WeChat ecosystem
  • Your staff need to clock in, carry out inspections and submit reports on the move, without being tied to a location
  • You want to send promotional notifications and order status updates to increase customer loyalty
  • Your existing website falls short and you want to offer a smoother mobile experience

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I build an App or a WeChat mini program?

If your customers mainly use WeChat, a mini program costs less to develop and is easier to promote; if you need complex features, offline use or long-term brand building, an App is a better fit. We will advise based on your target users and budget.

Can it integrate with our existing backend systems?

Yes. We connect the App or mini program to your existing POS, CRM, ERP, booking or membership systems through APIs, with data synchronised in real time.

How is the development timeline and cost calculated?

It depends on feature complexity. A mini program generally takes 4–8 weeks, and an App generally 8–16 weeks. Pricing is quoted based on features, design and the number of systems to be integrated, and we will provide a detailed proposal first.

Can you help with maintenance after launch?

Yes. We provide post-launch technical support, feature updates, server maintenance and app store review assistance to keep your application running reliably.
