Frequently asked questions

My drive failed. Will the data definitely come back?

Not always. Logical damage — deletion or accidental formatting — has a high success rate; physical damage is harder. We assess for free and quote afterwards, and there is no charge if nothing is recovered.

Is my data kept confidential?

Yes. All customer data is treated as confidential, and any working copy on our side is erased on request once the job is done. We regularly work with clinics and law firms.

Do I have to supply my own drive for a backup?

Yes, a drive of matching capacity. If you don't have one we stock Seagate, WD and Samsung drives.

I'm replacing my NAS. How do I move the data?

We handle NAS migration: MOP 300 for 2-bay, MOP 400 for 4-bay and MOP 500 for 5–6 bay, including setup and verification.
