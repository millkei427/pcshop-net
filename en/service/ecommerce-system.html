Why Open an Online Store?

  • Customers are used to checking prices and ordering online first, then picking up or having items delivered
  • An extra 24-hour sales channel beyond your physical shop
  • Online and in-store inventory get out of sync, often leading to overselling or missed orders
  • You want to run member offers, points and promo codes, but your current platform cannot support them
  • You want to reach markets beyond Macau, such as Hong Kong and Mainland China

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the online store sync with my existing POS or inventory system?

Yes. We design the integration around your existing systems, so stock is deducted automatically after each online order and order data is synced to your POS or ERP, avoiding duplicate entry and overselling.

Which payment methods are supported?

We can integrate MPay, credit cards, bank transfer, WeChat Pay, Alipay, PayPal and other channels commonly used in Macau, configured around your customers' habits.

Can you build a WeChat Mini Program or a mobile App?

Yes. Besides traditional online stores, we also develop WeChat Mini Programs, Alipay Mini Programs and mobile Apps so your customers can order anytime.

How long does it take to develop an online store?

It depends on the scope of features; most projects go live in 4–10 weeks. A standard online store (products, cart, payment, members) takes about 4–6 weeks, while projects integrating multiple back-office systems or custom logistics take about 8–12 weeks.
