Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a subscription model and a package model?

With a subscription, fees are charged on a recurring basis (for example a monthly fee) and the membership stays active; with a package, the customer pays once for multiple sessions, which are then deducted one by one. The same system can support both models at the same time.

How does automatic renewal work?

The system automatically generates an invoice or charges the customer according to the cycle you set (for example monthly), and sends a notification before each renewal. Customers can check their status or cancel at any time in the member centre.

Can I set an expiry date for packages?

Yes. For each package you can set how many days it remains valid after purchase, the interval between uses, whether it is transferable and other rules, and the system automatically reminds the customer before expiry.

Which payment methods can be supported for collecting payments?

We can integrate the payment channels commonly used in Macau, such as MPay, credit cards, bank transfer, WeChat Pay and Alipay, configured according to your customers' habits.
