Frequently asked questions

Where exactly in Hang Wan Kok is PC Shop?

Shop BG, on the ground floor. Enter the mall from Avenida do Ouvidor Arriaga and go down to the ground level. Full address: Shop BG, Hang Wan Kok Mall, Avenida do Ouvidor Arriaga, Macau.

There are several computer shops in the mall. Why this one?

PC Shop has traded in Macau for over a decade and, besides retail, provides repair, on-site IT support, network engineering, CCTV and POS systems — useful if you would rather buy and be supported by the same company.

Are you open on Sunday?

No — we are closed Sundays and public holidays. Opening hours are Monday to Saturday, 10:30–19:30. You can message WhatsApp 6392 8613 any time and we will reply when we open.

Can I try a machine before buying?

Yes. Laptops, desktops, monitors and gaming gear are on display and you are welcome to try them. If something you saw online isn't on the shelf, message us first and we will check.

Do I need an appointment to bring in a repair?

No appointment needed — just come during opening hours. Diagnosis and quotation are free, and most repairs are finished the same day or within two working days.

Need in-store sales & technical support?

Free diagnosis and quotation — we only start once you approve. Send us the model or a photo on WhatsApp and we can usually tell you straight away.
