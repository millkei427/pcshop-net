Common HR Pain Points for SMEs

  • Rosters scattered across WhatsApp, Excel and paper, leading to shift clashes and missed shifts
  • Inconsistent rules among staff for calculating sick leave, annual leave and time-off-in-lieu balances
  • Overtime hours recorded verbally, causing frequent disputes at month-end reconciliation
  • Nobody remembers when non-resident workers' Blue Cards or contracts expire, disrupting manpower planning
  • Admin staff stay up late checking rosters before payroll — one mistake means redoing everything

Aligned with Macau Regulations

We configure the system in accordance with Macau's Labour Relations Law (Law No. 7/2008) and related regulations:

  • Probation period, weekly rest day, mandatory holidays, annual leave and maternity leave rules
  • Overtime compensation rates and rounding rules
  • Severance compensation calculation
  • Access control under the Personal Data Protection Act (Law No. 8/2005)

Before go-live, we run trial calculations on your data from the past one to two months and check item by item to ensure accuracy.

FAQ

Does the system comply with Macau labour law?

We configure leave, weekly rest days, mandatory holidays, annual leave, maternity leave and severance compensation in accordance with Macau's Labour Relations Law (Law No. 7/2008), and verify them with trial calculations on historical data before go-live.

Can it handle shift work and overtime?

Yes. The system supports rostering, overnight shifts, late arrivals and early departures, and overtime hour statistics, with overtime compensation rates and rounding rules configured according to company policy.

How do Blue Card expiry alerts work?

The system records each non-resident worker's Blue Card expiry date, contract expiry date and probation end date, and can automatically remind the responsible colleague 30/60/90 days in advance so renewals are never missed.

How many employees make an HR system worthwhile?

As a rule of thumb, 15 or more employees, or more than 20 hours a month spent on payroll, makes it worth considering. Companies with heavy shift work, high turnover, or a mix of local and non-resident workers see the greatest benefit.
