Which businesses need an appointment scheduling system?

If your business needs to allocate staff, rooms or equipment by time slot, you need one:

  • Beauty / Hair / Nail / Massage:Customers choose the therapist, room and treatment time slot
  • Fitness / Yoga / Coaching:Group class sign-up, personal training slots, equipment booking
  • Medical clinics / Physiotherapy:Doctor and therapist rosters, registration, follow-up reminders
  • Education centres / Courses / Interest classes:Student enrolment, classroom allocation, teacher time slots
  • Photo studios / Meeting rooms / Venue rental:Facility time-slot management, payment confirmation

Core Features

📅 Multi-channel booking

Customers can book through a dedicated booking page, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook or a Google embed, and staff can also book on their behalf from the back office.

🪑 Resource scheduling

Staff, rooms, seats and equipment share one calendar, automatically preventing double bookings and showing available time slots in real time.

🔔 Automatic reminders

Automatic WhatsApp / SMS / Email reminders before each appointment reduce no-shows and lost slots.

💳 Deposits and payments

Set a partial or full deposit per service, with a cancellation policy. Integrates with payment methods commonly used in Macau such as MPay, credit cards and bank transfers.

👤 Membership data sync

Appointment records are written to the member profile automatically, making it easy to follow spending habits, treatment progress and re-marketing.

📊 Operations reports

Booking rate, cancellation rate, staff performance and peak hours at a glance, helping you adjust rosters and promotion strategies.

FAQ

Which types of businesses is an appointment system suitable for?

It suits any industry that needs to allocate staff or space by time slot, including beauty, fitness, medical clinics, education and course centres, photo studios and meeting room rentals.

Through which channels can customers book?

Bookings can be made through a dedicated booking page, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook or a Google embedded booking form, or staff can book on the customer's behalf from the back office.

Can the system collect deposits?

Yes. You can set a partial or full deposit for each service, together with a cancellation policy, to reduce losses caused by last-minute cancellations.

Will it conflict with my existing POS or membership system?

No. We design the integration around your existing systems, for example syncing appointment data to POS checkout, membership CRM or Google Calendar, to keep your workflow smooth.
